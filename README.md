## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard using the AdventureWorks dataset to analyze business performance across sales, products, customers, and geographic regions. The report provides actionable insights into revenue, profitability, order trends, product performance, and customer segmentation.

## Key Features
- **Executive Dashboard:** Tracks total revenue, total orders, total profit, and return rate, with monthly performance trends and product-category comparisons.
- **Geographic Analysis:** Visualizes order distribution across countries and continents using an interactive map.
- **Product Performance:** Analyzes product-level sales, revenue, and profitability against configurable targets. Includes dynamic metric selection and price-adjustment analysis.
- **Customer Analytics:** Evaluates customer acquisition trends, average revenue per customer, order behavior, income levels, and occupation-based segmentation.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Incorporates decomposition trees, key influencers, and Power BI Q&A to support deeper exploration of business drivers.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Enables users to explore performance by year, continent, product metrics, and customer metrics.

## Business Analysis & Key Insights

The dashboard analyzes AdventureWorks sales data across four major business dimensions: overall performance, geographic distribution, product performance, and customer behavior.

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Evaluated total revenue, total profit, order volume, and return rate to understand overall business performance.
- Analyzed monthly revenue, order, and return trends to identify changes in sales activity over time.
- Compared monthly performance against previous periods to support trend monitoring.
- Examined order distribution across product categories to understand category-level contribution to sales.

**Business insight:** Helps stakeholders identify changes in revenue and order activity, monitor returns, and recognize product categories contributing to overall business performance.

### 2. Geographic Sales Analysis
- Analyzed the geographic distribution of orders across countries and regions.
- Used map-based visualization to explore differences in sales activity by location.

**Business insight:** Helps identify key geographic markets, understand regional sales distribution, and highlight locations that may require additional business attention.

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
- Evaluated product-level revenue, profit, and order performance.
- Compared monthly orders, revenue, and profit against defined targets.
- Analyzed profit and return trends over time.
- Used dynamic metric selection to compare product performance across different business measures.

**Business insight:** Helps identify high-performing products, monitor target achievement, evaluate profitability, and investigate products associated with higher returns.

### 4. Customer Analysis
- Analyzed customer purchasing trends and order behavior over time.
- Segmented orders by customer income level and occupation.
- Identified and compared the top 100 customers based on selected performance metrics.
- Used customer-level analysis to investigate revenue contribution across customer segments.

**Business insight:** Helps identify valuable customer segments, understand purchasing patterns, and recognize high-revenue customers. For example, the report highlights Ruben Suarez as the highest-revenue customer among the Skilled Manual occupation segment in 2022, generating **$4,683**.

### 5. Advanced Business Analytics
- Implemented Power BI Key Influencers to investigate factors associated with selected business outcomes.
- Used Decomposition Trees to drill into metrics across product categories, subcategories, and individual products.
- Incorporated Power BI Q&A to enable natural-language exploration of business data.

**Business insight:** Enables stakeholders to move beyond high-level KPIs, investigate potential performance drivers, and explore the dimensions contributing to business results.

### Overall Business Value
The dashboard transforms sales and customer data into an interactive analytical reporting solution. It supports KPI monitoring, product profitability analysis, customer segmentation, geographic exploration, and deeper investigation of business performance drivers.
